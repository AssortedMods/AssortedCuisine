# Assorted Health

Adds sweets, bandages and health packs. Sweets are a quick snack made from sugar or powered sugar. Bandages and health packs take a moment to put on and then heal you straight away.

Also included in [Assorted Cuisine](../cuisine). Worlds made with Assorted Cuisine 1.x keep everything when you switch to this mod.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).

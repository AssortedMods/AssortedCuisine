# Assorted Kitchen

Adds cheese, butter, chocolate, eggs, sandwiches and five kinds of pie. The butter churn, cheese maker and chocolate bar mould turn milk and hot chocolate into something to eat, and the knife, whisk and mortar and pestle stay in the crafting grid and wear out as you use them.

Also included in [Assorted Cuisine](../cuisine). Worlds made with Assorted Cuisine 1.x keep everything when you switch to this mod.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).

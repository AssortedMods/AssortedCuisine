# Changelog

## 2.0.1

- Every food and drink now gives the saturation its food value was written for, as vanilla food does. They were being given a fraction of it: a cheese burger gave 0.95 and now gives 22.8, cooked eggs gave 0.8 and now give 16
- The hunger each food restores is unchanged

## 2.0.0

Updated to Minecraft 26.3, for NeoForge and Fabric.

- Requires Assorted Lib 5.0.0

## 1.0.0

- First release as its own mod. It used to only be part of Assorted Cuisine
- Worlds from Assorted Cuisine 1.x keep all of these blocks and items
- The chocolate pie can always be made now since the chocolate and the pies are in the same mod
- Requires Assorted Lib 4.3.0

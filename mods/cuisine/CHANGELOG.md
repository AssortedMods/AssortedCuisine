# Changelog

## 2.0.0

- Split into four mods that can also be installed on their own. Assorted Kitchen, Assorted Sodas, Assorted Health and Assorted Dragon Fruit
- Assorted Cuisine still includes all of them
- Worlds from 1.x keep all your blocks, items, recipes and advancements
- Each part can be turned off again in config/assortedcuisine-parts.toml
- Each mod has its own config file now, so settings from assortedcuisine-common.toml need setting again
- Requires Assorted Lib 4.3.0

## 1.0.0

First release, for Minecraft 26.2 on NeoForge and Fabric.

Grim Pack's Grim Cuisine part, ported from 1.12 and modernized. All six of its subparts are here -
dairy, chocolate, pies, health, soda and dragon fruit.

- Requires Assorted Lib 4.1.0.
